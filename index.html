About this study

This study examines how different AI explanation formats influence how people evaluate the credibility of online news-style content, revise their judgments, and report confidence during AI-assisted misinformation evaluation.

Why some content may have seemed misleading

Some of the news-style items shown in the study contained misleading or false content, and some AI outputs shown during the task may also have been incorrect. This was necessary because the study investigates not only whether people follow AI advice, but whether different interface designs support more careful and appropriately calibrated judgment when AI can be right or wrong.

Your data

Taking part was voluntary, and you can stop at any time before submitting your responses.

After submission, you may request withdrawal of your personal data within one month by contacting the researcher. If your responses have already been fully anonymized and can no longer be linked to you, it may no longer be possible to remove your individual data.
